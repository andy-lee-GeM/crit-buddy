# Standard Pipe Dimensions - Schedule 10/10S

Source: ASME B36.10M / B36.19M

**All dimensions in centimeters (cm).**

## Pigtails (NPS < 1/2)

Small diameter pipes used for pigtail connections.

| NPS   | OD    | ID    | Wall  | Ro    | Ri    |
|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| 1/8   | 1.029 | 0.780 | 0.124 | 0.514 | 0.390 |
| 1/4   | 1.372 | 1.041 | 0.165 | 0.686 | 0.521 |
| 3/8   | 1.715 | 1.384 | 0.165 | 0.857 | 0.692 |

**Conservative wall thickness (thinnest): 0.124 cm** (from NPS 1/8)

## Excluded Sizes

NPS 1/2 and 3/4 are excluded as they fall between pigtail and cascade categories:

| NPS   | OD    | ID    | Wall  | Ro    | Ri    |
|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| 1/2   | 2.134 | 1.712 | 0.211 | 1.067 | 0.856 |
| 3/4   | 2.667 | 2.245 | 0.211 | 1.334 | 1.123 |

## Notes

- **Ri** = Inner radius (fissile region boundary)
- **Ro** = Outer radius (wall outer boundary)
- **Wall** = Wall thickness = Ro - Ri
- For criticality safety, use the **thinnest wall** assumption (conservative)
- Schedule 10S is the thinnest standard schedule for stainless steel pipes
